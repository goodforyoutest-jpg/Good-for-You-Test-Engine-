Anti-Cheat & Exam Integrity

GFYTE includes built-in anti-cheat mechanisms to protect the integrity of assessments. During a live test, the platform monitors and logs:

  • Tab switches, window minimisation, and attempts to leave the test window.
  • Page reloads during an active test session.
  • Device and session information at the time of the attempt.

Repeated violations (currently set to 4 incidents) will trigger automatic submission of the test. All violation logs are made available to the assigned teacher and organisation admin.

Students acknowledge that these measures are in place when they begin any live test. Teachers and admins are responsible for communicating this to students under their organisation.

Privacy Policy
This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, who it belongs to, and what your rights are. We are committed to maintaining strict confidentiality for all organisations on the platform.
1
Data Ownership & Organisation Isolation
All your data — tests, results, and member information — belongs exclusively to your organisation. It is isolated and accessible only within the boundary of your unique organisation code. No person from another organisation can access, view, or retrieve your data in any form.

This isolation is enforced at the database level. Every record stored on the platform — test papers, student answers, results, member accounts — is tagged to your organisation code. Queries from one organisation's account cannot return data belonging to another.

Teachers can only access data within their own organisation. Students can only access tests and results tied to their own account within their organisation. Admins have full access within their organisation only.

2
What Data We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the platform. This includes:

  • Account information: Name, email address, role (student/teacher/admin), and organisation code — provided during account creation.
  • Test content: Questions, options, and correct answers uploaded by teachers or admins.
  • Test responses: Answers submitted by students, timestamps, time taken, and attempt metadata.
  • Result data: Scores, rankings, section-wise performance, and violation logs (tab switches, reloads).
  • Device information: Basic device/browser details logged at the time of a test attempt, used for integrity monitoring only.
  • Authentication tokens: Managed securely by Firebase Authentication; we do not store raw passwords.

We do not collect payment card details directly — these are handled entirely by our third-party payment processor (Razorpay), which maintains its own security and compliance standards.

3
No Selling or Sharing of Data
We do not sell, rent, or trade your data or your organisation's data to any third party. We do not use your data for advertising purposes. We maintain strict confidentiality between all client organisations on the platform.

Your data is never shared with other organisations using GFYTE. What happens inside your institute stays inside your institute.

The only limited circumstances under which data may be accessed beyond your organisation are:

  • Platform maintenance: The platform manager (Gajanan Dhobale) may access data in aggregate or specific form solely for the purpose of debugging, fixing issues, or maintaining the platform on your behalf.
  • Legal requirement: If we are required by law or court order to disclose certain information, we will comply but will notify you to the extent permitted by law.
4
How We Use Your Data

Data collected on the platform is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Authenticating and managing user accounts within your organisation.
  • Storing and displaying test content, student responses, and results.
  • Generating performance statistics, rankings, and reports within your organisation.
  • Detecting and logging exam integrity violations for review by teachers and admins.
  • Managing subscription and billing status for your organisation.
  • Improving platform stability, performance, and reliability.

We do not run analytics that profile individual students for any purpose outside your organisation's own reporting.

5
Data Deletion When Leaving the Platform
Important: If your organisation decides to leave the platform, we strongly advise you to notify us during your current active billing period — not after it ends — so that we can initiate the deletion of all member authentication data and account records we hold for your organisation.

We store member account information (names, emails, login credentials) to allow your members to sign in. If your organisation does not inform us before leaving, this data may remain stored on our systems.

Upon receiving a formal deletion request from your organisation's admin:

  • All member authentication records tied to your organisation will be permanently deleted.
  • Login access for all members under your organisation code will be revoked.
  • Test content and result data will be deleted as per your request (full deletion or partial retention for your own records — your choice).

After deletion is complete, nothing from your organisation will remain on the platform. To submit a deletion request, contact us using the details at the bottom of this page or through the Profile section in your account.

6
Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. These include:

  • Database-level security rules that enforce per-organisation data isolation.
  • Firebase Authentication for all login flows — passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • HTTPS-only access to all platform pages and API endpoints.
  • Restricted access to production systems — only the platform manager has administrative access.

No system is perfectly secure. While we do our best, we cannot guarantee absolute security against all possible threats. In the event of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you promptly and take corrective action.

7
Cookies & Session Storage

GFYTE uses browser session storage and authentication tokens to keep you signed in and to pass information between pages (for example, which test you are about to take). This is purely functional — we do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Firebase Authentication may set its own authentication cookies as part of its standard login mechanism. These are necessary for the platform to function and cannot be disabled while using the service.

We do not use any third-party advertising or analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics, Meta Pixel).

8
Students & Minors

GFYTE may be used by students who are minors, given its focus on educational assessments. In such cases, student accounts are created and managed by the registered organisation (teacher or admin) — not by the students themselves.

Organisations are responsible for ensuring they have appropriate consent from students or their guardians before enrolling them on the platform, in accordance with applicable laws and their own institutional policies.

We collect only the minimum data needed to operate a student's account (name, email, results) and we do not use student data for any purpose outside of operating the assessment platform.
